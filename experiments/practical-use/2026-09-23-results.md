walkthrough. The second is one fresh, preselected external case. These results
show two accepted applications and no refusal in this selected pair; they do
not estimate representative reliability across repositories or task classes.

then succeeded at the exact baseline before the task was submitted. Docker was
already running. Active operator and review minutes were not timed separately;

inspection. Task token usage and monetary cost were unavailable, not zero.
No semantic revision was requested. Later exits from the ready prompt did not
change the retained promoted outcomes. The immediate evaluation improvement is
